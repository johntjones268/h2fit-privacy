H2FIT Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

H2FIT ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights. H2FIT is available on both iOS and Android.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: Name, email address, password (stored as a secure hash), military rank, grade, unit, and MOS.
  • Personal information: Date of birth and gender, used solely to calculate age and gender-appropriate Army Fitness Test (AFT) scores.
  • Workout data: Workout type, duration, distance, and GPS route coordinates recorded during activity tracking.
  • AFT scores: Army Fitness Test results you manually enter.
  • Location data: GPS coordinates are collected only while you are actively recording a workout. Recording continues while the app is in the background so tracking is not interrupted if your screen locks during a workout. We do not access your location at any other time, and we never track your location when you are not recording a workout.
  • Health and fitness data: With your explicit permission, H2FIT reads health and fitness data from Apple Health (on iOS) or Health Connect (on Android). Depending on the platform and what you allow, this may include: steps, exercise/active minutes, active energy burned (calories), heart rate, resting heart rate, sleep, walking and workout distance, and workouts (including workout routes). You choose which categories to share, and you can revoke this access at any time in the Apple Health app, the Health Connect settings, or your device settings.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide workout tracking and AFT scoring features
  • To display your activity in the in-app social feed visible to other H2FIT users
  • To calculate age and gender-appropriate AFT performance scores
  • To display Army Fitness Test (AFT) rankings so users can see standings and compare scores across their pay grade, a selected unit, or the entire organization (see "Leadership, Command, and Fitness Test Rankings" below)
  • To power your Performance dashboard, which summarizes recent daily metrics such as steps, exercise minutes, resting heart rate, sleep, and calories
  • To provide unit leaders and commanders with readiness metrics for the units they oversee (see "Leadership and Command Visibility" below)
  • To maintain your account and authenticate you securely

How Health Data Is Stored and Synced

When you grant permission, H2FIT periodically syncs your recent health and fitness metrics (typically the last 30 days of daily summaries, plus imported workouts) from Apple Health or Health Connect to our servers. This allows your Performance dashboard and readiness reporting to work across sessions and devices. We only read the categories you approve; we do not write data back to Apple Health or Health Connect.

We do not use health or fitness data for advertising, and we do not sell or share it with third parties for their own purposes. Our use of Apple Health and Health Connect data complies with the applicable Apple Health and Google Health Connect requirements, including restrictions on the use and disclosure of health data.

Leadership, Command, and Fitness Test Rankings

H2FIT is a unit readiness tool, so some information is visible beyond your own account:

  • Your Army Fitness Test (AFT) scores are visible to other H2FIT users. The app includes fitness-test rankings that let users see standings and compare AFT scores — for example across your pay grade, a selected unit, or the entire organization. Your name, rank, unit, and AFT score may appear in these rankings.
  • Authorized leaders and commanders may see aggregate unit metrics (such as unit-wide averages, AFT pass rates, and participation) for the units in their chain of command, through the H2FIT administrator portal.
  • Other individual health details — such as heart rate, sleep, steps, and daily activity synced from Apple Health or Health Connect — are limited to a soldier's own commander and to H2FIT system administrators, and are used for readiness purposes.

This visibility supports unit health and readiness. Access to individual health data (other than AFT standings) is role-based and scoped to a leader's own unit.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or trade your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own commercial purposes. Within the app, your workout data (name, workout type, distance, duration, and GPS route) is visible to other H2FIT users in the app feed, your Army Fitness Test scores are visible to other users through in-app rankings, and your readiness information is visible to your unit leadership — all as described above. You can delete your account at any time from the Profile tab, which permanently removes your data from our systems.

Data Security

Passwords are stored using bcrypt hashing and are never stored in plain text. Authentication uses industry-standard JWT tokens. All data is transmitted over HTTPS.

Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You may delete your account at any time through the app's Profile tab, which will permanently remove your personal information from our servers.

Your Choices

Health and fitness sharing is optional and off until you enable it. You can grant or revoke H2FIT's access to Apple Health or Health Connect at any time from the Apple Health app, the Health Connect settings, or your device's privacy settings. Revoking access stops future syncing; you can also delete your account to remove previously synced data.

Children's Privacy

H2FIT is intended for use by adults (18 and older). We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes through the app. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
